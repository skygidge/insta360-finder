## Learnings

**What worked:** Returning to academic paper search via new channels (Frontiers in Communication, ERIC educational database, PubMed). This run successfully found stories in completely new domains: journalism (Spain) and film education (UK) — the first non-medical/non-marine stories in several consecutive runs.

**What didn't work:** Week C niche community searches were entirely unproductive. DPReview forums, Vimeo, Filmmaker Magazine, No Film School, Reddit filmmaker communities (r/Filmmakers, r/videography, r/documentaryfilmmaking), ScubaBoard, and non-English searches (Spanish/French) all returned zero verifiable creator stories with confirmed Insta360 use.

**New sources found:** ERIC educational research database (eric.ed.gov) — useful for education research papers that cite specific Insta360 camera models in methods sections.

**Confirmed exclusions this run:** Rory Sheldon ("The African Wild") — already on Insta360 enterprise blog; Lance Fenderson (adaptive racer) — already an official Insta360 partnership; Telmo dos Reis ("Close Encounters") — 2020 project, too old; Laurent Portes (Madagascar) — no confirmed Insta360 use; Robbie Shone (cave photography) — no confirmed Insta360 use.

Priority: Google Scholar specifically for 'Insta360 X5' (released 2025, likely appearing in 2025-2026 papers); journalism/documentary communities (NPPA, Emmy Award databases, immersive journalism researchers); ThingLink platform for Insta360-using educators like Dr. Millea.
