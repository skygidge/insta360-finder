- National Geographic "How I shot it" / "How a photographer captured" articles are a reliable, unmined source for professional Insta360 use on editorial assignments. Marcus Westberg confirms this pattern.
- IndieWire publishes SEPARATE camera surveys for narrative features (not just documentaries) at Sundance and SXSW — both surveys confirmed Insta360 use in festival films this run.
- Searching for specific film titles ("Magic Farm" insta360, "LifeHack" insta360) is more efficient than broad festival + camera queries.

- ISPRS/Copernicus PDFs consistently return 403.

**Confirmed exclusions this run:**
