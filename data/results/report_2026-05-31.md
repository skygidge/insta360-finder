## Story 1 — WSL Forest Team, Switzerland *(Confidence: Medium)*

**Researchers:** Hristina Hristova, Clemens Blattert, Bogdan Candrea, Janine Schweier
**Institution:** Swiss Federal Institute for Forest, Snow and Landscape Research (WSL), Birmensdorf, Switzerland
**Product:** Insta360 Pro 2
**Source type:** Peer-reviewed academic journal (Ecological Informatics, September 8, 2025)
**Source link:** https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1574954125004078

### Summary
A WSL research team used an Insta360 Pro 2 camera to capture 360-degree video transects of forest plots for automated 3D reconstruction via videogrammetry. Their method — published in Ecological Informatics on September 8, 2025 — achieved tree position errors of 5.2–18.8 cm and DBH errors of 0.9–1.9 cm, demonstrating that a single-operator walk with an Insta360 Pro 2 can produce point clouds rivalling terrestrial LiDAR for forest inventory purposes.

### Why it matters for Insta360
Swiss federal scientists proved the Insta360 Pro 2 can produce scientifically credible 3D forest data at a fraction of LiDAR cost — opening a significant professional/government market for the camera.

## Story 2 — REPLACE Project, University of Essex *(Confidence: Low-Medium)* ⚠️ RECENCY FLAG

**Researchers:** Paola Di Giuseppantonio Di Franco, Francesca Dolcetti, Matteo Baraldo
**Institution:** University of Essex, School of Philosophy and Art History, Colchester, UK
**Product:** Insta360 ONE RS (1-inch 360 edition)
**Source type:** Peer-reviewed academic journal (Visual Communication, SAGE, 2025)
**Source link:** https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/14703572251364346

### Summary
Researchers on the UKRI Future Leader Fellowship project REPLACE (Rebuilding a Sense of Place) used an Insta360 ONE RS (1-inch 360 edition) mounted on a 120 cm selfie stick to conduct walking interviews with residents in Amatrice and Accumoli, Italy — communities devastated by the 2016 earthquake. Over 20 hours of 360-degree ethnographic footage was captured. A methodology paper was published in Visual Communication (SAGE) in 2025; the project has multiple other 2025 publications and remains active.

### Why it matters for Insta360
Post-disaster cultural heritage documentation with an Insta360 ONE RS, published in a UKRI-funded academic project with ongoing 2025 output — a compelling social science use case.
